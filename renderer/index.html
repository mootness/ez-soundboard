🎧 Discord setup

Discord hears your microphone, not your speakers. A free virtual audio cable (VB-Cable) carries the soundboard into Discord as if it were a microphone — and EZ Soundboard can mix your real mic into it too.

Closing the window keeps EZ Soundboard running in the tray, so Discord still hears you. If you quit it, Discord won't hear your mic. Use headphones for the Monitor so your mic doesn't pick up the clips.

🔊 Volume

Each clip is measured once and played at the same loudness. Tile and master volumes go up to 200% on top of that, and a limiter stops loud settings from distorting.

Sent to Discord
💬 In Discord: Settings → Voice & Video
  1. Input Device: the VB-Cable one, "CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)"
  2. Noise Suppression: None. Discord's treats sound effects as noise and turns them down.
  3. Echo Cancellation and Automatic Gain Control: off
  4. Input Sensitivity: turn off "Automatically determine" and drag the slider to the far left

With "Send my microphone" on, the soundboard cleans up your mic itself, so turning these off doesn't let background noise through.
